D.1 Recorded change

Under protocol §§3.5 and 3.7, contrasts from the same study system may be non-independent because they share a population, location, disturbance history, or research programme. The primary model captures study-level dependence through ref_id and shared ancestry through the phylogeny, but does not include a separate study-system term. In PROCEED, sys_id, ref_id, and species identity are strongly associated. Most systems map to one species and often one source, so fitting all three grouping structures can make their variance components difficult to distinguish.

NoteWhat changed—and what did not

Changed: a study-system random intercept, (1 | sys_id), was added.

Unchanged: the full dataset, moderator definitions, phylogenetic effect, ref_id effect, priors, and MCMC settings match the primary models.

The comparison uses the fixed-effect tables. The three variance components are not reported separately.

D.2 Model specification

Models were fit on totoro, not while rendering this book: 4 chains × 4,000 iterations (2,000 warmup), adapt_delta = 0.97, max_treedepth = 15, and the cmdstanr backend.

Code 
phylo   <- prepare_phylo_and_data(dat_mod, A_full)
formula <- build_ls_formula(moderator, has_phylogeny = phylo$has_phylo,
                            has_sys_id = TRUE)
fit     <- fit_ls_model(
  phylo$dat_model, formula,
  build_ls_priors(formula, phylo$dat_model, phylo$V, A = phylo$A_mod),
  V = phylo$V, A = phylo$A_mod, mcmc_args = default_mcmc_args)

D.4 Overall comparison

The comparison label uses a fixed rule: DIFFERS means that the posterior median changed sign or that the 95% credible interval changed between including and excluding zero. Consistent means neither occurred; it does not mean the two estimates are identical. Intercepts are omitted from the overall count.

Code 
cat("**Overall:** ", sens_stability_summary(variant), "\n")

Overall: 7 of 36 moderator coefficients change their credible-interval conclusion relative to the primary fit.
